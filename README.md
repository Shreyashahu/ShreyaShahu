## Highlighted Projects

### 🛰️ Flood Impact & Crop Stress Detection, Bhandara, Maharashtra
*Maharashtra Remote Sensing Application Centre · Sentinel-1/2, GPM IMERG, Google Earth Engine*
- SAR change-detection algorithm that mapped a **24.3 km²** peak flood extent and **49.3 km²** of cumulative inundation
- Same-date flooded vs. non-flooded NDVI comparison showing **13.8%** of cropland (331 km²) stressed; rainfall correlated with flood extent (R = 0.935)

### 🏥 Sepsis Phenotyping and Health Equity in Critical Care ML
*MIMIC-IV, eICU-CRD · Python, BigQuery, SHAP*
- Four sepsis phenotypes with mortality ranging **29%–68%** from K-Means on 5,888 ICU patients
- Creatinine removal reassigned 5.7% of Black vs. 2.8% of White MIMIC-IV patients; no difference in eICU
- Accepted at DABCon-2026 · [Preprint](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10508429/v1)

### 🧬 Network-Guided Transcriptomics for CAD Severity Classification
*Whole-blood RNA-seq · Python, scikit-learn, Enrichr*
- Six-stage pipeline reducing **28,278 genes to 55 features**; Random Forest one-vs-rest AUCs of 0.69–0.86 under LOOCV, after detecting and correcting target leakage
- Under review, STAR Global Conference · [Repository](https://github.com/Shreyashahu/cad-transcriptomics)

### 🌾 ClaimEasy: Geospatial Crop Insurance Claim Validation
*Northern Lights Analytics · FastAPI, Sentinel-1/2*
- Cross-checks farmer-submitted images (EXIF date and GPS) against satellite imagery and weather data, deployed as a FastAPI service with automated PDF reports
